The reasons your SEO isn't moving the numbers

Most SaaS teams don't have a traffic problem. They have a pipeline problem dressed up as an SEO report. Here's where it usually breaks down.

Rankings that don't convert

You rank for dozens of terms, but almost none of them bring in a trial or a demo. Traffic is up and to the right; pipeline is flat.

CAC that climbs every quarter

Paid works until the budget review. Every new dollar buys a lower-intent click, and your blended acquisition cost creeps up with it.

Content that reads but doesn't sell

You publish every week and it ranks. Then the reader hits the end of the post and there's nowhere to go — nothing tied to the product.

Invisible in AI search

Buyers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews for a shortlist. Your competitors get named. You don't get mentioned at all.

No proof it's working

Leadership wants revenue, not rankings. Without attribution, SEO looks like a cost center and its budget is the first thing on the chopping block.

No bandwidth to execute

Keyword strategy, technical fixes, content and links — all at once, at scale — is more than a lean team can carry. Things slip and momentum dies.
