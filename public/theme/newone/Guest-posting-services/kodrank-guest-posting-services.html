Answers

Guest posting, without the guesswork

What exactly are guest posting services?
Guest posting services handle the full process of getting your brand published on other websites: finding relevant publishers, pitching them, writing the article, and placing a link back to your site inside the content. Done well, it earns editorial backlinks that build authority and send real referral traffic. KodRank runs all of it for you, and you approve the sites before anything goes live.
How is this different from buying cheap links?
Cheap links usually come from PBNs or link farms with fake traffic, and the link often sits in an author box that search engines ignore. Every KodRank placement comes from genuine outreach to a site with real readers, with your link placed inside the body of a human-written article. It's the difference between a link that helps and one that quietly puts you at risk.
Do I get to choose the websites?
Yes. We send you a shortlist of hand-picked publishers with live metrics — traffic, domain rating, and niche relevance — and nothing is written or placed until you approve them. You stay in control of exactly where your brand appears.
Who writes the content?
Real writers, not bots. Each article is written by a person to match the host blog's tone and audience, with your link woven in where it reads naturally. Every piece is reviewed by an editor before it goes live, so it holds up to the publisher's standards and yours.
What if a link gets removed later?
If a published link ever drops, we replace it at no extra cost. You're never left paying for a placement that disappeared, and you won't have to chase it — we monitor and handle the replacement.
Can agencies white-label these guest posting services?
Absolutely. Our guest posting services are built for resale. Every campaign comes with a clean, unbranded report of live URLs and metrics that you can forward straight to your client — they'll never know KodRank was involved.
How long until I see results?
Placements typically go live within 7–14 days of site approval. Ranking movement depends on your niche, competition, and existing profile, but because these are real editorial links on trafficked sites, they tend to hold and compound rather than spike and fade.
